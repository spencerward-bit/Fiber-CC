Delete your Color Optics account

This page is the public account-deletion resource for Color Optics on the web and in the Android app. You can delete the account yourself while signed in. Deletion is permanent.

Open the account page Privacy Policy

Delete it on the website

Open the Color Optics account page and sign in with the account email and password. Choose Delete account, type DELETE, and confirm. You do not need the Android app to do this.

Delete it in the Android app

Open Color Optics, sign in, and open Account from the header. Choose Delete account, type DELETE, and confirm.

If you cannot sign in

Email the support address shown on the Color Optics Google Play listing from the mailbox on the account. Include the account email and the words “delete my Color Optics account.” The account and the data listed below will be deleted after the request is verified. You can also use that same support address for a deletion request if the in-account button is unavailable.

What is deleted

The Color Optics sign-in is deleted, along with saved reference settings stored for that account and the account’s subscription record in Color Optics. An active web subscription is canceled first so it does not renew. A copy of the saved settings on a device is cleared by the app after a successful deletion; settings that were only stored in another browser can be removed by clearing that browser’s site data.

What may be kept

Payment and invoice records already held by the payment processor can be retained for accounting, tax, and fraud-prevention obligations. Those records are not used to keep the Color Optics account active. Color Optics does not keep the deleted account’s email login or saved reference settings after the deletion finishes.
